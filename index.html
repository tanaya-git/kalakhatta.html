How to serve

  1. Chill the bottle for at least two hours.
  2. Pour over crushed ice in a tall glass.
  3. Finish with a lime wedge and a pinch of black salt.

Questions

How long does a bottle last?

Unopened, nine months in a cool place. Once opened, keep it in the fridge and finish within seven days.

Is it sweet?

It is balanced. The sourness and salt keep it from tasting heavy.
